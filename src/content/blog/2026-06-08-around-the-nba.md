## Knicks' Magical Run

The New York Knicks are on fire, folks! They're making history with their incredible playoff and Finals run, reminiscent of the 2004 Detroit Pistons. Charles Barkley noted that the Knicks play as a complete collective unit, rely on stifling defense, and win by having a deep group of players who understand their roles rather than depending on one superstar.

> "The New York Knicks' incredible playoff and Finals run reminds him of the 2004 Detroit Pistons. He noted that, like the legendary Pistons, the Knicks play as a complete collective unit, rely on stifling defense, and win by having a deep group of players who understand their roles rather than depending on one superstar." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

Their best moment came when they dominated Game 2, showcasing their impressive win streak. As [Jorge Sierra](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=) pointed out, the Knicks now have the best point differential in NBA playoff history, surpassing the 2013-14 Spurs and 2016-17 Warriors.

## Spurs' Struggles and Wemby's Growth

Meanwhile, the San Antonio Spurs are trying to regroup after a tough loss in Game 2. [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) thinks the Spurs need to punch the Knicks in the face and see if their magic disappears. He also mentioned that Wemby and the Spurs talking about winning the championship after the OKC series made his ears perk up, and now they need to back it up.

> "When I heard Wemby and the Spurs talking about winning the championship after the OKC series it made my ears perk up. Maybe a language thing but it sounded like they thought the hard work was done. They expected a coronation, they got a back room brawl." - [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404)

## NBA Rumors and Moves

In other news, the Oklahoma City Thunder are facing some difficult roster decisions, including team options for Hartenstein, Dort, and Williams. However, [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) reported that trading Chet Holmgren is not part of the plan.

The Cleveland Cavaliers are also dealing with roster issues, particularly with Dean Wade and Keon Ellis, as they try to move out of the second apron. And, in a surprising move, Jalen Brunson's trainer revealed that he's been working on a drill called the "Wemby" to improve his arch.

## Mavs Fans React

Our MavsBoard community is buzzing with reactions to the current NBA landscape. [Dirknows](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=732) wasn't impressed with Wemby's emotional display after winning the WCF, while [ballsrchr](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=71) thinks Brunson would excel with a team like the Knicks.

> "Yeah, did not really get the Wemby crying after winning the WCF thing. Spurs look worn down from the 7 game OKC series. Could never root for the Spurs so hope Brunson can bring it home for New York." - [Dirknows](https://://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=732)

## Predictions and Takeaways

As the Finals continue, Mavs fans are eager to see how the series unfolds. Will the Knicks maintain their dominance, or can the Spurs bounce back? One thing's for sure - the NBA is more exciting than ever, and MavsBoard will be here to bring you all the latest updates and discussion.

In a final note, [SwisherPrice](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=254) had a scathing critique of Keldon Johnson's performance: "Keldon Johnson is one of the worst 6th Man of the Year winners ever. Sure, I guess he deserved it based on stats but in terms of playing style, he's really not that good of an offensive player and these playoffs have exposed him." 

The Knicks' next game will be crucial in determining the series outcome, and we'll be watching closely to see if they can keep their magic alive.

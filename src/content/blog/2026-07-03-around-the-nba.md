## The Blazers' Dark Past
The Portland Trail Blazers' tumultuous history was on full display recently, with [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) sharing a fascinating anecdote about owner Robert Dundon's thoughts on the team's infamous "Jail Blazers" era. According to HoosierDaddyKid, Dundon was disgusted by the way fans turned on their team during that time, saying, "The players didn’t do anything. Indeed, they were not caught on film waving around a handgun in a Colorado nightclub."

This got me thinking: what would happen if the Mavs had a documentary series about our own team's history? Would we be reliving the glory days of Dirk and the crew, or would we be exposing some skeletons in the closet? 

## Coaching Carousel
The NBA coaching world was abuzz recently, with some big names making moves. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) pointed out that Billy Donovan is joining the Spurs as an assistant coach, which raised some eyebrows: 

> "Odd move. Billy has been an HC in the NBA for years. I wouldn't think he would seek to be an assistant for his next job." 

However, as [Scott41theMavs](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=97) quipped, this move might be more about Mitch Johnson's job security than anything else: 
> "What? That’s not odd at all. It’s called, “Mitch, you suck, but we like you as a person too much to fire you yet, so  here is.” 

## Eastern Conference Outlook
[F Gump](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=412) took a look at the upcoming season and thinks the East is going to be a real battle: 
> "With all the big moves, there should be some really good teams next season, and to me the ones who look like they have positioned themselves the best are mostly in the East.. It looks like a major fight ahead just to get a top-6 playoff spot."

He's got some strong contenders in mind, including the Knicks, 76ers, and Raptors. But what about the teams on the outside looking in? Can anyone make a push for the playoffs?

## Devin Booker's Jersey Switch
In a bit of news that's both cool and confusing, [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared that Devin Booker is changing his jersey number from 1 to 15: 
> "Shams Charania: Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin, per ESPN sources."

As [omahen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=17) pointed out, this might have some implications for Mark Williams, who's a free agent and currently wears number 15 for the Suns.

## Luka's Contract Conundrum
[michaeltex](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=23) raised some concerns about Luka Doncic's upcoming contract situation: 
> "His next Max is going to put him in the neighborhood of $70M/yr on average and the LALs would be committing ~$360M over 5 years."

This is a problem we Mavs fans are all too familiar with – what happens when your superstar player wants (and deserves) a max contract? Do you make the sacrifices necessary to keep them, or do you try to build around them in other ways?

## Nuggets Outlook
[Knutsen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=963) questioned whether the Nuggets really improved this offseason: 
> "Do you feel like Denver improved? They haven’t signed Watson yet, lost Hardaway, and Bagley won’t make any difference - and are historically cheap and will lose a player or two."

[F Gump](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=412) thinks the Nuggets still have a legit shot at a title, though: 
> "I think the Nuggets have a legit MVP-level star to carry them, and a lot of very useful players around him."

## The Never-Ending Commercial Break
[RoyTarpleysGhost](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=881) hit a universal pain point: 
> "I will say the last 2 minutes of a NBA game take forever.  I’ve rage quit watching some games after they go to commercial break like 7-8 times in the last two minutes of a game."

Can we all just agree that commercial breaks are the WORST?

## A Look Back at History 
As [DallasMaverick](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=352) put it: 
> "If you’re a player, winning an NBA championship is very uncertain, regardless of what financial sacrifices you might make to facilitate roster-building.  So don’t."

Words to live by, Mavs fans! As we look ahead to the next season, let's keep things in perspective. And who knows – maybe someday we'll be celebrating our own championship win (sans drama, fingers crossed).

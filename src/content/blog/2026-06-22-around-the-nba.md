## Devin Booker's Jersey Switch
Devin Booker is changing his jersey number from 1 to 15, a nod to his USA Basketball uniform and his dad Melvin's college number. What's interesting is that Mark Williams, a free agent this season, currently wears No. 15 in Phoenix. Maybe this is a sign that Williams won't be back with the Suns?

> "Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin" - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Luka's Requirements
Luka Doncic had some interesting comments on what he needs around him to succeed. He emphasized the importance of shooters and a center who can run the pick-and-roll. No surprise there, but it's always good to hear the star player himself say it.

> "I think mainly shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll and jump so I can pass to him. If I have shooters, they won't double me as much, and it helps me out." - Luka Doncic

## The Knicks' Parade and Jalen Brunson's Pay Cut
The Knicks' parade drew over 2 million people to lower Manhattan, a testament to their exciting season. Meanwhile, Jalen Brunson's pay cut has sparked debate. Some think it's a great move, while others believe it's a mistake. 

> "The idea that any player making a contractual sacrifice automatically leads to success does not align with what I have seen repeatedly." - Bernie Lee

## Gary Payton's Street Naming
Naismith Basketball Hall of Famer Gary Payton had a street named after him in his hometown of Oakland. A well-deserved honor for "The Glove."

## Coaching Carousel
The Spurs' coaching staff is taking shape, with additions like DJ Bakker and Riley Crean. The Blazers' coaching search includes Chris Jent, the Knicks' associate head coach.

## Myles Bridges on Jalen Brunson
Myles Bridges had some interesting comments on Jalen Brunson, saying that Becky Hammon's comments actually motivated him. Maybe Hammon's comments will be a good thing for Brunson in the long run?

> "I appreciate (Becky Hammon), no cap. The words she said about can't win with (Jalen Brunson) being a '1A', '1B', 'he too little', all that did is fuel him bro." - Myles Bridges

## Conference Realignment
The NBA might be realigning conferences, with teams like Memphis, Minnesota, and New Orleans competing to move to the Eastern Conference. A big change could be on the horizon.

## FireNicoHarrison's Takes
[FireNicoHarrison](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=4633) had some hot takes on Smart, Ayton, and the Lakers. He thinks Smart is overrated and not a championship-level player. 

> "Smart & Kennard are not Championship level players, not starters for sure. Maybe 10/15minutes role players." - [FireNicoHarrison](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=4633)

As the NBA offseason heats up, one thing is clear: Mavs fans have opinions. And who knows, maybe next season will bring some surprises. But one thing's for sure - Luka will be looking for those shooters and a big man to run the pick-and-roll with.

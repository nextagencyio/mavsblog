## Devin Booker's New Number

The NBA offseason is heating up, and we're not just talking about the draft and free agency. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared some news about Devin Booker changing his jersey number from 1 to 15. 

> "Shams Charania: Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin, per ESPN sources." 

Booker's dad, Melvin, wore the number 15 in college, and Booker also rocked it during his USA Basketball days. It's a cool nod to his heritage and family history.

## Coaching Carousel

The NBA coaching carousel is spinning out of control, and [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) has got the scoop. 

> "Marc Stein: Dallas’ hiring of Dusty May leaves Portland as the NBA’s only team without a head coach entering Tuesday’s draft … with sources saying Knicks assistant Chris Jent is getting real consideration for that post alongside finalists Micah Nori (Wolves) and Tyler Lashbrook (Celtics)." 

It looks like the Trail Blazers are finally filling their coaching vacancy. And the winner is... Micah Nori! [Mavs2021](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504) thinks it's a solid hire.

> "Nori is 52 years old. He has been an assistant for 17 years. I find this completely reasonable. He probably gets paid more than he did as an assistant in the last 17 years and he gets a chance to prove whatever he got as a head coach." 

## Luka's Priorities

Luka Doncic is one of the most dominant players in the league, and he knows what he needs to succeed. 

> "Luka Dončić says he wants shooters and a big man he can run pick-and-rolls with

“I think mainly shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll and jump so I can pass to him. If I have shooters, they won’t double me as much, and it helps me out. I think I always need shooters around me because I usually get double-teamed a lot, so I think I need shooters—and some centers who can jump high and block shots.” 

Simple enough. Give Luka some shooters and a big man who can roll with him, and we'll see some magic on the court.

## Jalen Brunson's Pay Cut

[Jalen Brunson](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) took a pay cut to stay with the Knicks, but [Bernie Lee](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) isn't buying into the idea that it's a guarantee for success.

> "The idea that any player making a contractual sacrifice automatically leads to success does not align with what I have seen repeatedly. Once a player sacrifices money, he is handing a tool back to an organization to make decisions he does not control, while still carrying the results of those decisions." 

It's a fair point. A pay cut doesn't necessarily mean a team will gel or win a championship.

## Knicks Parade

The Knicks just celebrated their championship win with a parade, and [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared some wild stats.

> "Mike Vorkunov: More than 2 million people came out today to lower Manhattan for the Knicks parade, according to the NYPD" 

That's a lot of people celebrating a championship. Hopefully, the Mavs can join that party soon.

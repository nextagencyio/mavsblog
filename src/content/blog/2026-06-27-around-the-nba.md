## Devin Booker's Jersey Switcheroo
Devin Booker is ditching his iconic No. 1 jersey for No. 15, a nod to his USA Basketball uniform and his dad's college number. 
> "Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin" - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## A Familiar Number in Phoenix
Interestingly, Mark Williams, a current free agent, rocks the No. 15 jersey in Phoenix. 
> "Interesting connected information: Mark Williams, who is a FA this season, currently wears No.15 in Phoenix" - [omahen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=17)

## Luka's Ideal Teammates
Luka Doncic shared his thoughts on what makes a team click: shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll. 
> "I think mainly shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll and jump so I can pass to him. If I have shooters, they won’t double me as much, and it helps me out." - Luka Doncic 

## Portland's New Coach
The Blazers have found their man in Micah Nori, formerly an assistant coach with the Timberwolves. 
> "Micah Nori has agreed to become the head coach of the Portland Trail Blazers, sources tell @TheAthletic" - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Knicks' Championship Parade Shenanigans
The Knicks' parade was a wild ride, with over 2 million people in attendance. However, one woman took things too far, stealing a trash can and getting fired from her JPMorgan Chase job. 

## Jalen Brunson's Pay Cut
Jalen Brunson's decision to take a pay cut with the Knicks sparked debate.

## MavsBoard Member Insights

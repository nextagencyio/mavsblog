## The Wolves' Offseason Ahead

The Minnesota Timberwolves' playoff run may have been exciting, but it seems like their magic has worn off. As [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) noted, "I think the last three years they were not one of the top 2 teams in the west but they made two finals. I think this years run was probably closer to what they are." The Wolves have an interesting offseason ahead of them, with a mid-to-late pick that could be perfect for a point guard. 

> "I really liked Shannon jr. I think him and Ayo (if resigned) can be a nice rotation." - [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404)

## Wemby Watch

Meanwhile, Victor Wembanyama is already making waves in the league. The Spurs' rookie sensation has been dominating on defense, with [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) sharing that Wemby has the 2nd most blocks (41) in the 1st ten games of his first playoff series, behind only Dikembe Mutombo (62) since blocks were started being recorded in 1974.

> "Wemby has the 2nd most blocks (41) in the 1st ten games of his first playoff series behind Dikembe Mutombo (62) since blocks were started being recorded in 1974." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Timberwolves' Trade Rumors

The Wolves may be looking to shake things up this offseason, with [RoyTarpleysGhost](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=881) imagining their two offseason objectives: getting a PG and getting Julius Randle off the team. 

> "I imagine their two offseason objectives are to 1. get a PG 2. get Julius Randle off the team They need a CP3 type player. I would be surprised if they didn't trade for a veteran PG." - [RoyTarpleysGhost](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=881)

## Kyrie to the Wolves?

The Wolves may be looking to add a big name to their roster, with [Knutsen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=963) wondering if Kyrie Irving would be the one piece to make them a serious competitor.

> "Wouldn’t Kyrie be the one piece to make them a serious competitor? Is there a package that we’d be interested in or would it be a three way deal?" - [Knutsen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=963)

## Mavs Fans React

As a Mavs fan, it's always interesting to see what other teams are up to. While we may not be directly involved in these trades and rumors, it's always fun to speculate. And who knows, maybe we'll see some movement in the coming weeks.

## The NBA's Referee Problem 

In other news, [Unknown](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=0) shared that an anonymous survey of 411 players conducted by and released today by @TheNBPA, showed that NBA referees have been placed in three rating tiers. 

## Conclusion 

The NBA offseason is always full of surprises, and it seems like the Wolves are just one of many teams with a lot to figure out. One thing's for sure: with Wemby on the rise and the Wolves looking to shake things up, the Western Conference is going to be interesting next season. And as [CooPerFan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=7574) joked, "Just for content tbh". We'll see what the future holds!

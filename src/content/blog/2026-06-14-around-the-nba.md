## The Spurs' Stumbling Block

The Spurs' loss in the Finals has Mavs fans and NBA enthusiasts weighing in on their growth and potential. [cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92) kicked off the discussion, questioning if this Spurs team is "the most unclutch team in finals history." The Spurs' inability to close out games, leading by double digits in all five games late in the game and winning only one, has raised concerns about their ability to perform under pressure.

> "Is this the most unclutch team in finals history?" - [cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92)

## Jalen Brunson's Championship Win

Congratulations are in order for former Mavs player Jalen Brunson, who led his team to a championship win. [Mavs03](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=460) gave a nod to Brunson, saying, "Congratulations to former Dallas Maverick Jalen Brunson. Who would have thought he would win a championship before Luka." Brunson's impressive performance, including a 45-point game, earned him the respect of his peers and opponents alike.

> "That's a hell of a group. That's a well-coached (team). The culture they have is clearly evident. They have it." - Jalen Brunson on the Spurs

## Victor Wembanyama's Growth

The Spurs' young star, Victor Wembanyama, has shown immense potential, but some are questioning his ability to be a closer. [GATA](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=1044) chimed in, saying, "Wemby isnt it, man. He will never be a closer. Generational defense, but he's never going to be a top 10 offensive player." However, others are optimistic about his growth, with [Unknown](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504) stating, "Agreed, and frankly, his shooting touch from all over the court is already better than I expected it to be in year 7."

> "He's going to be something special for a lot of years to come. Once-in-a-generation athlete." - Karl-Anthony Towns on Wemby

## The Knicks' Championship Win

The Knicks' championship win has significant implications for the NBA landscape. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared some interesting stats, including the fact that the Spurs outscored the Knicks by 5.4 points per 100 possessions with Dylan Harper on the court during the NBA Finals. The Knicks' win also raises questions about their status as a desirable destination for free agents.

> "Shout out to the Spurs, too. They're the real deal. Shout out to Jersey's own, Dylan Harper; he's the real deal." - Karl-Anthony Towns

## Mavs Fans React

As Mavs fans, we're still reeling from our team's recent struggles. [Dirknows](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=732) kept it light, saying, "I’m not gonna shed any tears over the Spurs losing in the finals." Meanwhile, [SwisherPrice](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=254) offered a hot take, suggesting that the Spurs' loss might be beneficial for Victor Wembanyama's growth.

> "This was a must-lose series if you're against the Spurs. Wemby will probably win multiple championships throughout his career...if they won it now in only Year 3 for him, they would probably go through a Bulls/Jordan run." - [SwisherPrice](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=254)

As the NBA offseason heats up, Mavs fans will be keeping a close eye on the Spurs' growth and the Knicks' roster moves. One thing's for sure – the 2026 NBA Finals will be remembered for years to come. And who knows? Maybe next year, we'll be celebrating a Mavs championship. A fan can dream, right?

## Double-Teaming SGA: A Futile Effort?

The Oklahoma City Thunder are giving the NBA a run for their money, and it's all thanks to Shai Gilgeous-Alexander (SGA). **HoosierDaddyKid** notes that double-teaming SGA hasn't worked thus far in the playoffs. 
 > "Double-teaming SGA hasn't worked thus far in the playoffs." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Bench Scoring Woes

The Spurs and Thunder series has been a nail-biter, with both teams struggling to find consistency. **HoosierDaddyKid** shares a concerning stat: 
 > "Bench points through the first three games. SA/OKC" 

## Coaching Moves and Staff Changes

In a bit of coaching news, God Shammgod joins Jamal Mosley's staff in New Orleans. 
 > "God Shammgod joins Jamal Mosley's staff in N.O." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Knicks on a Tear

Meanwhile, the New York Knicks are on a 10-game winning streak, setting an NBA record for point differential (+234). **HoosierDaddyKid** gives credit to Mike Brown: 
 > "I have to give Mike Brown his flowers. (I wasn't a fan of his as a coach) The Knicks are on a 10-game winning streak and set an NBA record for point differential in the process (+234)" 

## Wemby's Growth Pains

The San Antonio Spurs' Victor Wembanyama is having a rough series against the Thunder. [hakeemfaan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=499) notes that Wemby got hacked a lot in Game 2 without anything being called: 
 > "It's easier said than done, because the NBA moved in a different direction a long time ago. Even Shaq for all his blather about how he would dominate now, couldn't keep up with the officiating changes once he wasn't allowed to just push and clear folks off the blocks." 

## OKC's Salary Cap Woes

The Thunder's salary cap situation is a concern, according to [windjc](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=308): 
 > "In today's NBA that is not financially sustainable... The point is as F Gump pointed out they have no money to pay him." 

## Mavs Fans Troll Spurs

[Mavs fan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=592) wants the Spurs to get rid of **Castle** asap: 
 > "Castle sucks, in fact I'll give some free advice to any potential Spurs front office types that may be reading this: get rid of this guy asap, preferably to a team you don't like, such as the Mavs." 

The playoffs are heating up, and Mavs fans are keeping a close eye on the competition. One thing's for sure: the Knicks are on fire, and Wemby needs to step up his game. Will OKC's salary cap woes catch up to them, or will SGA lead them to a championship? Only time will tell. Until then, Mavs fans will be watching from the sidelines, eager for their team's turn in the spotlight. And who knows? Maybe someday, we'll see a Luka Doncic and Victor Wembanyama duo dominating the league. A Mavs fan can dream, right?

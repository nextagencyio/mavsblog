## The Knicks' Championship Hype

The Knicks are on the doorstep of their first championship in 27 years, and Mavs fans are both thrilled and annoyed. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) can't stand Knicks fans, saying, "Knicks fans are disgusting. If they win it all, they better call in the National Guard." Meanwhile, [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) thinks James Dolan, the Knicks' owner, is getting too much glory: "Bad owners make everything hopeless...  If you have enough money and immune to cr, you can live the life." Dolan's been a terrible owner, but he's sitting pretty with a potential championship.

> "I haven't liked Dolan since he had Charles Oakley kicked out of MSG during a playoff game. How do you do that to one of the most popular players in Knicks history?" - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Spurs' Physicality and Wemby's Growth

The Spurs' young core, led by Victor Wembanyama, is making waves in the playoffs. [numnuts23](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=573) thinks the Spurs' guards have been allowed to play too physically: "The grabbing and holding at the top has been over the top." Meanwhile, [michaeltex](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=23) is concerned about Wemby's behavior: "I've always thought Wemby was a spectacular, once in a generation, talent. But I have to say my appreciation of his game has been tarnished by some of the thuggery he's displayed during these playoffs."

> "If Wemby can stay healthy then yes they’ll be perennial contenders. Most 7-6 guys aren’t able to stay healthy." - [Dirknows](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=732)

## Mavs Fans React

Mavs fans are excited about their team's future, with [cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92) saying, "Even without Wemby, I'd say the future is pretty bright with Castle/Harper and that combo can let Wemby not have to log major minutes in the regular season which should help him stay healthier." [Mavs2021](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504) is confident in Luka Doncic's abilities: "I just don´t see a Luka led Mavs losing to these teams. F*** Nico."

## Masai Ujiri's Drafting Prowess

[Cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92) and [mvossman](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=482) discuss Masai Ujiri's drafting skills, with [mvossman](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=482) saying, "Not in the same league, but I do think these are Masai kind of guys. Positional size (including massive wingspans) good defenders and not great shooters." [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) thinks Ujiri's late draft picks have turned into stars: "I am going to think of the two players Masai drafted late who have been two of the best players in the finals in consecutive years."

## Conclusion

As the NBA Finals heat up, Mavs fans are reacting to the Knicks' championship hype, Spurs' physicality, and Wemby's growth. With the Mavs' own young core on the rise, fans are excited for the future. Will the Knicks take home their first championship in 27 years? Can Wemby and the Spurs make a deeper run next season? One thing's for sure – the MavsBoard community will be discussing it all.

## Devin Booker's Number Switcheroo
The NBA offseason is heating up, and we're not just talking about the draft and free agency. Devin Booker is making a change to his jersey number, switching from No. 1 to No. 15. According to Shams Charania, Booker is making the change to honor his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin.

> "Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin, per ESPN sources." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Luka's Shooter SOS
Luka Doncic had a clear message for the Mavs' front office: get me some shooters, please! In a recent interview, Luka emphasized the importance of having shooters around him to space the floor and prevent double-teams.

> "I think mainly shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll and jump so I can pass to him. If I have shooters, they won't double me as much, and it helps me out." - [Unknown](https://www.mavsboard.com)

## The Blazers' Coaching Conundrum
The Portland Trail Blazers are still searching for a head coach, and it seems like they're considering some big names. Chris Jent, an assistant coach for the Knicks, is reportedly getting consideration for the job. Meanwhile, Micah Nori has agreed to become the head coach of the Blazers, signing a one-year deal with team options for the next two seasons.

> "Micah Nori has agreed to become the head coach of the Portland Trail Blazers, sources tell @TheAthletic. Nori was lead assistant of the Minnesota Timberwolves for the past five years." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Jalen Brunson's Pay Cut Conundrum
Jalen Brunson's decision to take a pay cut with the Knicks has sparked some debate. Bernie Lee, a well-respected NBA writer, argues that players making contractual sacrifices doesn't necessarily lead to success.

> "The idea that any player making a contractual sacrifice automatically leads to success does not align with what I have seen repeatedly. Once a player sacrifices money, he is handing a tool back to an organization to make decisions he does not control, while still carrying the results of those decisions." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Mavs Miscellany
The Mavs' coaching staff is starting to take shape, with DJ Bakker, Mfon Udofia, and Riley Crean joining as assistant coaches. And in a bit of news that's both hilarious and disturbing, a woman was caught on video stealing a public trash can during the Knicks' championship parade – and it turns out she was a director at JPMorgan Chase.

## The Smart Debate
[FireNicoHarrison](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=4633) is having none of it: "DPOY is trash." They're referring, of course, to Marcus Smart, the former Defensive Player of the Year who's now with the Memphis Grizzlies. We're not taking sides here (okay, maybe we are), but it's clear that Smart's value is a topic of debate.

As the NBA offseason heats up, one thing is clear: it's going to be a wild ride. And we're excited to see what the Mavs do next. Will they land some shooters to help Luka out? Only time will tell. But one thing's for sure: we'll be here, cheering them on and trash-talking their opponents.

## NBA Viewership on the Rise

The NBA playoffs have seen a significant surge in viewership this season, with NBC averaging 7.2 million viewers for its return to the playoffs, up 72% from last season. As [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) points out, nine of the top 10 and 17 of the top 25 most-watched playoff games this season were on NBC, led by the Spurs-Thunder Game 7 in the Western Conference Finals, which drew 15.9 million viewers.

> "NBC averaged 7.2 million viewers for its return to the NBA Playoffs this season, up 72% vs. the comparable coverage last season." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

The NBA Finals have also seen impressive viewership numbers, with Game 1 averaging 16.9 million viewers, the most-watched Game 1 since 2018. The game peaked at 19.63 million viewers in the fourth quarter, a testament to the excitement and drama of the series.

## Wemby's Blunder and the Knicks' Dominance

The Knicks are on a serious roll, with 13 straight wins and 9 straight on the road. Their dominant performance has left fans and critics alike wondering if they can go undefeated in the playoffs. However, [CooPerFan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=7574) points out that Wemby's critical turnover in the game was a turning point:

> "Wemby with that boneheaded critical turnover after getting that defensive rebound was the primary they lost the game." - [CooPerFan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=7574)

## KAT's Strong Performance and Brunson's Off Night

KAT has been playing exceptionally well, leading some to wonder if he should be considered for Finals MVP. [cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92) notes that KAT has been great, while Brunson had an off night, especially in the 4th quarter.

> "As much as I hate to admit it, KAT has been great." - [cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92)

## The Spurs' Struggles and Wemby's Development

The Spurs' loss has sparked debate about Wemby's development and his ability to lead a team to a championship. [Knutsen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=963) suggests that Wemby could learn from Dirk's playstyle, operating from the elbow and using his length to his advantage.

> "Just imagine Wemby with his length operating like Dirk from the elbow. He wouldn’t even need to fade to be unblockable with his size." - [Knutsen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=963)

## The 82-0 Simulation Game

In a lighter note, [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shares a viral basketball simulation game, 82-0.com, where players try to build the ultimate historical NBA starting lineup and see if it can go undefeated at 82-0 in the regular season.

## Conclusion

As the NBA playoffs heat up, Mavs fans are weighing in on the action. From Wemby's blunder to KAT's strong performance, there's no shortage of opinions on the series. With the Knicks on a 13-game win streak and NBA viewership on the rise, it's an exciting time for basketball fans. As [SwisherPrice](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=254) notes, the Knicks have all the tools to neutralize the Spurs, including depth, size, experience, and shooting.

Will the Knicks continue their dominance, or will the Spurs bounce back? One thing is for sure - it's going to be a thrilling conclusion to the playoffs. And as [CooPerFan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=7574) jokingly points out, Wemby losing in the finals means he'll never surpass Jordan as the GOAT... or will he?

## Harden's Next Chapter

The NBA world is abuzz with James Harden's latest move - heading to Cleveland. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) kicked off the discussion, noting that the Cavs went all-in on Harden, and we'll see what that brings on Sunday. 
> "Cleveland went all in on Harden. We'll see what that brings on Sunday. I'm still surprised Toronto is in this series without Quickley and Ingram."

Meanwhile, [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) isn't convinced Harden will lead the Cavs to victory: 
> "I am sure Clev fans are super confident . I actually think he plays pretty good in game 7 and the Cavs win. The Harden stinker is coming later in the playoffs is my guess."

## Jokic's Future in Denver

[HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) also shared some insight on Nikola Jokic's future in Denver, courtesy of Shams: 
> "Shams on Jokic's future in Denver."

And in a somewhat related note, MPJ had some interesting comments on the Nuggets' trade decision: 
> "MPJ says the Nuggets would have won if they hadn't traded him."

## Philly's Magical Run

The 76ers just pulled off an incredible upset, and [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) had the details: 
> "Philly took advantage of no Tatum and Boston missing 12 of their last 13 shots in the final 5 minutes, having Embiid playing well along with PG, Maxey, and Edgecombe was the difference."

[Knutsen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=963) is loving Philly's chances going forward: 
> "Philly is dangerous, they are getting the band together at the right time. A starting five of Maxey, Edgecomb, George, Oubre and Embiid is very solid on both ends of the floor."

## Mavs Rumblings

In a discussion that caught our attention, [RoyTarpleysGhost](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=881) brought up the possibility of Anthony Davis joining the Blazers: 
> "There's a legitimate chance that Anthony Davis is traded by the Wizards before appearing in a game for the franchise. The Trail Blazers, who previously pursued him at the trade deadline, could be a potential landing spot— especially if they fail in their chase of Giannis Antetokounmpo."

The Mavs were apparently not interested in a deal involving Jerami Grant and draft capital. 
> "It was Jerami Grant, expiring contracts, and a heavily protected pick for Anthony Davis, and it never gained any traction whatsoever."

## A Familiar Face?

Could James Harden be a Mav someday? [Scott41theMavs](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=97) posed the question: 
> "Maybe he's 'washed up' enough that the Mavs can get permission to talk to him."

As we wrap up this roundup, one thing's for sure - the NBA landscape is about to get a whole lot more interesting.

And with that, we'll leave you with a bold prediction: the 76ers will make a deeper playoff run than expected, and Joel Embiid will give us all a reason to go "The Process was right!"

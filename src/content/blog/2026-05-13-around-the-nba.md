## The Kidd-Nurse Swap

The MavsBoard community is buzzing about a potential coaching swap: Jason Kidd to the Philadelphia 76ers' front office and Nick Nurse to the Dallas Mavericks' bench. [DanSchwartzgan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=72) kicks off the discussion with a straightforward proposal:

> "Straight up swap...Kidd to the Philly FO and Nurse to the Dallas bench?"

[CooPerFan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=7574) seems to support the idea, but [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) cautions that the Masai Ujiri-Nurse relationship in Toronto didn't end well. Would Nurse be a good fit in Dallas? Only time will tell.

## LeBron's Uncertain Future

LeBron James' plans for a 24th season in the NBA are shrouded in mystery. When asked about his future, James replied:

> "When the time comes you guys will know."

[Dirknows](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=732) isn't buying it:

> "We know he wants the most extravagant farewell tour ever, why is he even playing."

The Lakers' offseason will be one to watch, with [omahen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=17) noting that they have a goal to build a contender, but their cap space is limited. Do they bring back LeBron and Rui Hachimura, or make a push for new talent?

## Luka's Family Drama

Luka Dončić's personal life made headlines recently, with the star player announcing he won't play for Slovenia's national team to spend more time with his daughters. [Unknown](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=0) shares the news:

> "The Lakers’ Luka Dončić announced tonight via Instagram that he will not play for Slovenia's national team this summer in FIBA World Cup qualifying to spend as much offseason time as possible with his two daughters as he continues to try to secure joint custody."

[Dirknows](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=732) feels for the Mavs superstar:

> "Yes I loved Luka playing for the Mavs. But the fact him and his fiancé broke it off when he had to move to LA and kept him from his kids is the bigger tragedy."

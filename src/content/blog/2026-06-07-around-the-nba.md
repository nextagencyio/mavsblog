## Knicks' Historic Run Continues

The New York Knicks are on a roll, folks! They just made history by becoming the third team to win Game 1 & 2 of the NBA Finals on the road. And let's not forget, they're heading into Game 3 with the second-longest single-season win streak in NBA playoff history. According to [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159), "ESPN Insights: The New York Knicks this postseason: ? 43 days since their last loss ? Third team to win Game 1 & 2 of NBA Finals on the road ? Heading into Game 3 on the 2nd-longest single-season win streak in NBA playoff history ? 8th straight road playoff win".

> "Best point differential in NBA playoff history: 2013-14 Spurs: +214, 2016-17 Warriors: +230, 2025-26 KNICKS: +282. A run unlike any other." - [Jorge Sierra](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404)

## Spurs' Collapse

Meanwhile, the San Antonio Spurs are trying to regroup after a tough loss. [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) thinks they need to "punch the Knicks in the face and see if that magic disappears". He also believes that Wemby and the Spurs might have gotten too confident after beating the OKC Thunder.

> "When I heard Wemby and the Spurs talking about winning the championship after the OKC series it made my ears perk up. Maybe a language thing but it sounded like they thought the hard work was done. They expected a coronation, they got a back room brawl." - [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404)

## Thunder's Future Plans

The Oklahoma City Thunder are facing some tough roster decisions, including team options for Hartenstein, Dort, and Williams. However, [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) reports that trading Chet Holmgren is not on the table.

> "He's the best defensive player in the league ... aside from one." - A source close to the Thunder

## NBA News

The NBA world is buzzing with news, from TV ratings to viral basketball simulation games. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shares that NBC's playoff coverage averaged 7.2 million viewers, up 72% from last season.

## Spurs' Bench Woes

The Spurs' bench has been a major concern, with [SwisherPrice](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=254) calling Keldon Johnson "one of the worst 6th Man of the Year winners ever". The Knicks' depth has been a key factor in their success.

> "Knicks just better. They have all of the tools to neutralize the Spurs...depth, size, experience, shooting. Their Big-4 is better than the Spurs Big-4. And the depth advantage that the Spurs were supposed to have has been meaningless because their bench has given them nothing outside of Harper." - [SwisherPrice](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=254)

## Wemby's Growth

Despite a tough loss, [Knutsen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=963) believes Wemby has huge potential.

> "I was writing this a couple of weeks ago - just imagine Wemby with his length operating like Dirk from the elbow. He wouldn’t even need to fade to be unblockable with his size." 

## Prediction

The Knicks are on fire, and it's hard to see them slowing down. But hey, the Spurs have shown resilience before. Will they bounce back in Game 3? Only time will tell.

As [Dirknows](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=732) says:

> "Yeah, did not really get the Wemby crying after winning the WCF thing. Spurs look worn down from the 7 game OKC series. Could never root for the Spurs so hope Brunson can bring it home for New York."

The Knicks are looking strong, but the Spurs won't go down without a fight. Bring on Game 3!

## Spurs' Agonizing Defeat
The Spurs' heartbreaking loss to the T'Wolves had Mavs fans like [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) feeling pretty good. I mean, who doesn't love watching the Spurs struggle without Gregg Popovich's mastermind on the bench?

> "I really dislike the T'Wolves but watching the Spurs lose because they don't have the IQ of Pop on the bench these days is a real feel good moment for Mavs fans. 7s left on the clock. Down 2. They don't call a timeout. And to make things worse they are slow to push the ball up the court. Still get a decent look from 3 but I don't even understand why you would go for the winner in the first place. If you call a timeout chances are that the Wolves overcommit to prevent a 3 and give up something closer to the rim." - [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14)

[Mavs2021](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504) shot back, "So your problem with the decision is that the Spurs did not make a wide open three. Gotcha." But [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) maintained that the Spurs should've gone for overtime instead of a potentially contested three.

## OKC's Juggernaut
The Oklahoma City Thunder's impressive playoff run has turned heads, and [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) is loving what he's seeing. "OKC is such a machine. You would think playing 11 in a playoff game would cause issues...this is missing their 2nd/3rd best player too. Nope, they just keep rolling."

> "If you squint just a little bit you can envision Ajay Mitchell being an all star. Just a second year former second rounder. They are just littered with guys acquired different ways. Just a handful of lottery picks they made." - [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404)

[omahen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=17) chimed in, praising the Thunder's coach and their ability to find talent: "It all starts with the coach - I think theirs is best coach in the league by a wide margin. They got several high picks and drafted well."

## NBA News and Rumors
The NBA world is buzzing with news, from Joel Embiid's injury to Luka Doncic's recovery. [Unknown] shared that Embiid has been ruled out for Game 2 due to ankle and hip injuries. Meanwhile, Luka Doncic has started running but hasn't been cleared for full contact.

> "Luka Doncic (hamstring) updates: - Said he's started running - Has yet to be cleared for full contact - Said his original timetable given to him was 8 weeks (his injury occurred about 5 weeks ago)" - [Unknown]

## Underrated and Overrated Players
In an interesting poll, NBA players voted on the most overrated and underrated players in the league. [Unknown] shared the results, with Alperen Sengun and Rudy Gobert making the list of most overrated players.

> "NBA players voted on the most overrated player in the league: • Alperen Sengun: 12.3% • Rudy Gobert: 8.6% • Trae Young: 8.6% • Karl-Anthony Towns: 7.4% • Paolo Banchero: 4.9% • Ja Morant: 4.9% • Deni Avdija: 3.7% • Scottie Barnes: 3.7% • Luka Doncic: 3.7%" - [Unknown]

On the flip side, [Unknown] shared the list of most underrated players, with Jalen Johnson and Derrick White receiving 4% of the votes.

## Mavs Fans' Takeaways
As the playoffs heat up, Mavs fans are keeping a close eye on potential targets for their team. [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) mentioned that he'd love to find an "Al Horford deal" for the Mavs.

> "I would love to find a Horford deal out there if I was the Mavs. Not sure there is one. With the new cap, maybe there are some things they can take advantage of with teams." - [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404)

And with that, the discussion continues. Will the Spurs bounce back next season? Can OKC keep their momentum going? One thing's for sure - Mavs fans will be keeping a close eye on it all.

## MavsBoard Roundup: AROUND the NBA

## The Blazers' Dark Past
The Portland Trail Blazers have been making headlines recently, but not for their on-court performance. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared an interesting anecdote about Mark Dundon, who apparently was disgusted by the way fans turned on their team during the "Untold: Jail Blazers" era. 

> "Five weeks ago, I sat across from Dundon in North Carolina, and he told me that he had watched 'Untold: Jail Blazers,' the Netflix documentary on the era that has defined modern Trail Blazers history, on a flight from Dallas to Raleigh. What he saw, he said, disgusted him. Not the flagrant and unapologetic drug use, the rape allegation or the bad attitudes, but the way fans turned on their team."

It's fascinating to see how different perspectives can shape one's opinion on a team's history. The Blazers' struggles have been well-documented, and it's clear that their past mistakes have had a lasting impact.

## NBA Offseason Moves
The offseason is in full swing, and teams are making big moves to strengthen their rosters. [F Gump](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=412) weighed in on the top teams in the East and West, giving his thoughts on which teams are poised to make a deep run.

> "With all the big moves, there should be some really good teams next season, and to me the ones who look like they have positioned themselves the best are mostly in the East.. It looks like a major fight ahead just to get a top-6 playoff spot: NY, PHI, TOR, MIA, ATL, CLE?, DET?, BOS?"

The West, on the other hand, seems to be a bit more top-heavy, with [F Gump](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=412) identifying the Spurs, Thunder, and Nuggets as the top contenders.

## Devin Booker's Number Switch
In a bit of news that's not directly related to the Mavs, [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared that Devin Booker is changing his jersey number from 1 to 15.

> "Shams Charania: Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin, per ESPN sources."

This move is a nice nod to Booker's heritage, and it's always cool to see players show appreciation for their roots.

## The Mavs' Future
Of course, Mavs fans are always interested in what's happening in Dallas. [michaeltex](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=23) had a interesting take on Luka Doncic's contract situation and how it might impact the team's future.

> "His next Max is going to put him in the neighborhood of $70M/yr on average and the LALs would be committing ~$360M over 5 years."

It's a good thing the Mavs have a talented young core to build around, because navigating the salary cap can be a real challenge.

## A Tribute to Gary Payton
In some feel-good news, [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared that Gary Payton had a street named after him in his hometown of Oakland.

> "Marc J. Spears: Naismith Basketball Hall of Famer @GaryPayton had a street named after him in his hometown @Oakland today."

It's always great to see former players get the recognition they deserve.

## A Cautionary Tale
Finally, [DallasMaverick](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=352) offered some sage advice on the idea of players making contractual sacrifices to facilitate roster-building.

> "If you're a player, winning an NBA championship is very uncertain, regardless of what financial sacrifices you might make to facilitate roster-building. So don't."

It's a good reminder that players should prioritize their own interests and not feel pressured to make sacrifices that might not pay off in the end.

As the NBA offseason heats up, Mavs fans will be keeping a close eye on the team's moves and how they might impact the league as a whole. One thing's for sure: it's going to be an interesting year. And if the Mavs can keep building on their young core, we might just see them make a deep run in the playoffs. But for now, let's just enjoy the ride. **The West is wide open, and the Mavs are ready to take on all comers.**

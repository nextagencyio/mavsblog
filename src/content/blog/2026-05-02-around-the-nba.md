## Harden's Next Move

The NBA world is buzzing with James Harden's latest move to Cleveland. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) kicked off the discussion, noting that the Cavaliers went all-in on Harden, and it's time to see what that brings on Sunday.

> "Cleveland went all in on Harden. We'll see what that brings on Sunday. I'm still surprised Toronto is in this series without Quickley and Ingram." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

[Hicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) chimed in, saying that Harden might just play well in Game 7, and the Cavs could win. However, he's predicting a stinker from Harden later in the playoffs.

## Jokic's Future in Denver

The Denver Nuggets are facing some uncertainty, and [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared some interesting news: 

> "MPJ says the Nuggets would have won if they hadn't traded him." 

Meanwhile, [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) discussed the Nuggets' potential offseason moves, including trading Aaron Gordon or Michael Porter Jr. to create cap space.

> "The two most likely, but uncomfortable, options are trading either Johnson and/or Gordon." - [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404)

## Historic Game 7s

This weekend is all about Game 7s, folks! The 76ers are taking on the Celtics, and the Magic are facing off against the Pistons. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) highlighted the importance of Boston's three-point shooting in their wins:

> "In the three games Boston has won, it has shot almost 42% from 3-point range. In the three games the Celtics have lost, they have shot under 30%."

The Cavaliers are also looking to make a statement against the Raptors, with [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) predicting a win for Cleveland.

## Kuminga's Future

The Atlanta Hawks' offseason is looking interesting, with another top-10 pick on the horizon. [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) discussed the possibility of drafting a guard and the implications for Jonathan Kuminga's future.

> "Kuminga has another year left on his deal if they want to pick it up? Sounds like the trade market will not be strong for him." - [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404)

## Gobert's Impact

The Minnesota Timberwolves' acquisition of Rudy Gobert has raised some eyebrows, with [michaeltex](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=23) asking if it validates the move:

> "Does this validate the acquisition of Gobert by MIN? I mean, not for nothin' did he get 4 DPOY." 

Some users are still excited about Gobert's potential, while others are skeptical about his fit in the modern NBA.

As we wrap up this roundup, one thing's for sure: the NBA playoffs are heating up, and MavsBoard is buzzing with discussion. Will the Cavaliers make a statement with Harden? Can the Nuggets find a way to keep Jokic happy? And what's next for the Hawks and Kuminga? Stay tuned for more updates from around the league!

And finally, as [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) so eloquently put it:

> "Denver has no real offense when Jokic isn't forcing the opposing defense to double him and the Wolves have one of the few bigs in the league that can at least contain him 1v1."

## Devin Booker's Jersey Switcheroo 

[HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) dropped some news: **Devin Booker is changing his jersey number from No. 1 to No. 15**. Apparently, it's a nod to his USA Basketball uniform and his dad Melvin's college number. 

> "Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin, per ESPN sources." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

Interesting side note: [omahen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=17) pointed out that Mark Williams, a current free agent, rocks the No. 15 jersey in Phoenix. 

## Luka's Blueprint 

Luka Doncic had some words of wisdom on what he needs to succeed: **shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll**. Simple, yet effective. 

> "I think mainly shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll and jump so I can pass to him. If I have shooters, they won’t double me as much, and it helps me out." - Luka Doncic 

## Knicks Drama 

The Knicks are making waves, and not just because of their parade. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared that **over 2 million people attended their parade** in lower Manhattan. But what's more interesting is the chatter around Jalen Brunson's pay cut. 

[Bernie Lee](https://twitter.com/bernieleee) had some thoughts on the matter: 

> "The idea that any player making a contractual sacrifice automatically leads to success does not align with what I have seen repeatedly. Once a player sacrifices money, he is handing a tool back to an organization to make decisions he does not control, while still carrying the results of those decisions." 

## Coaching Carousel 

The NBA coaching carousel is spinning, and [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) had some updates. **Chris Jent is getting consideration for the Blazers' coaching job**, alongside Micah Nori and Tyler Lashbrook. Meanwhile, **Dusty May's hiring in Dallas leaves Portland as the only team without a head coach**.

## Knicks' Championship and Title Gear Sales

The New York Knicks are your 2026 NBA Champions, and their title gear sales are going through the roof. According to [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159), Knicks championship gear has become the top seller across all sports within the first 24 hours of a title in Fanatics history. As [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) puts it:

> "Brown will have nba champion behind his name forever now nd we be one a few coaches who has led a team to a title."

Mike Brown's coaching prowess was on full display, and [F Gump](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=412) had some great insights on his strategies:

> "The Knick who impressed me the most in the Finals, vs my expectations, was not Brunson. It was Mike Brown. I had thought he was mostly a meh coach who got lots of chances because of his history as a SA assistant, and then would get fired when each place figured it out."

## Spurs' Collapse and Coaching Carousel

The San Antonio Spurs' collapse in the Finals was a hot topic of discussion. [Mavs03](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=460) didn't mince words:

> "Young or not, this was an absolute choke job by the Spurs.  They led by double digits in all 5 games late in the game and won only 1 game."

Meanwhile, [DallasMaverick](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=352) had a more nuanced take:

> "If not for a couple of mental lockups, inexperience and youth, we could easily be proclaiming the Spurs as the current and future dynasty of the NBA right now."

## NBA Rumblings and Realignment

The NBA is abuzz with rumors and potential changes. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared some interesting tidbits:

> "There is also the question of how the league will realign the conferences. Different possibilities are being discussed, including moving one team from the Western Conference to the East -- with Memphis, Minnesota and New Orleans all 'competing' to do so."

## Myles Bridges and Jalen Brunson

[Myles Bridges](https://www.mavsboard.com/) had some interesting comments about Becky Hammon and Jalen Brunson. According to [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159):

> "Bridges held an Instagram live session and said that he appreciates Hammon. He made it clear that Brunson knows her comments and that they motivated him more to silence his critics."

## DeAaron Fox's Contract and Potential Moves

The Sacramento Kings' [DeAaron Fox](https://https://www.mavsboard.com/) contract is raising some eyebrows. [RoyTarpleysGhost](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=881) wonders:

> "What kind of conversations are the Spurs are having internally about DeAaron Fox?  He's owed like $220 million over the next 4 years.  One of the worst contracts in the league?"

## A Look Ahead to the 2026-2027 Season

As the NBA offseason heats up, fans are already looking ahead to the 2026-2027 season. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared some early NBA Finals odds:

> "Early 2026/2027 NBA finals odds... Who's ready to make some bold predictions?"

The Mavs may not be in the championship conversation just yet, but with a talented young core, there's reason to be optimistic. As [Dirknows](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=732) quipped:

> "I’m not gonna shed any tears over the Spurs losing in the finals."

The NBA is full of surprises, and the Mavs are no exception. Stay tuned for more updates, analysis, and banter on MavsBoard!

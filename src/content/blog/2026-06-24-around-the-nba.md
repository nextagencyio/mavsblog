## Devin Booker's New Number

The NBA never sleeps, folks! Devin Booker is changing his jersey number from 1 to 15, inspired by his USA Basketball uniform and his dad's college number. 
> "Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin, per ESPN sources." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

That's a cool gesture, and who knows, maybe it'll bring him good luck. On a related note, Mark Williams, a free agent this season, currently wears No. 15 in Phoenix. Coincidence? 

## Luka's Needs

Luka Doncic had some interesting comments on what he needs from his teammates to perform at his best. 
> "I think mainly shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll and jump so I can pass to him. If I have shooters, they won’t double me as much, and it helps me out." - Luka Doncic

No surprise there - Luka's a genius on the court, and he knows what makes a team tick. Give him some shooters and a big man who can roll, and watch out! 

## Coaching Moves

The NBA coaching carousel is spinning, and the Portland Trail Blazers just found their new head coach in Micah Nori. 
> "Micah Nori has agreed to become the head coach of the Portland Trail Blazers, sources tell @TheAthletic . Nori was lead assistant of the Minnesota Timberwolves for the past five years." 

[Nori](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) gets a chance to prove himself as a head coach, and at 52, he's got some experience under his belt. 

## Jalen Brunson's Pay Cut

The Knicks' parade was a blast, but [FireNicoHarrison](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=4633) wasn't impressed. Meanwhile, Jalen Brunson's pay cut sparked a debate. 
> "The idea that any player making a contractual sacrifice automatically leads to success does not align with what I have seen repeatedly." - Bernie Lee

[DallasMaverick](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=352) chimed in, 
> "If you’re a player, winning an NBA championship is very uncertain, regardless of what financial sacrifices you might make to facilitate roster-building. So don’t."

Wise words, [DallasMaverick](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=352).

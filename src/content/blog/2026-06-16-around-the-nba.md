## The Knicks are Champions, Baby!

The New York Knicks are your 2026 NBA Champions, and Mavs fans are still trying to process the implications. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared some juicy tidbits, including the fact that each Knicks player will earn around $770K from the NBA for their title. That's a nice payday for guys like **Sochan** ($806K salary), **Diawara** ($1.3M), and **Alvarado** ($1.7M).

> "This team Leon Rose built is the NBA's first to win the Larry O’Brien Trophy and NBA Cup double in the same season. It just beat the Spurs four times in five tries despite spotting a double-digit lead to San Antonio in the first quarter of all five games." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Spurs, What Happened?

The Spurs' collapse in the Finals has left many Mavs fans scratching their heads. [Mavs03](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=460) wasn't mincing words: "Young or not, this was an absolute choke job by the Spurs. They led by double digits in all 5 games late in the game and won only 1 game." Ouch! [DallasMaverick](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=352) offered a more nuanced take, suggesting that the Spurs' inexperience and youth were major factors.

> "If not for a couple of mental lockups, inexperience and youth, we could easily be proclaiming the Spurs as the current and future dynasty of the NBA right now." - [DallasMaverick](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=352)

## NBA Realignment Rumblings

The NBA might be shaking things up with conference realignment, and [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) had the details: "There is also the question of how the league will realign the conferences. Different possibilities are being discussed, including moving one team from the Western Conference to the East -- with Memphis, Minnesota and New Orleans all 'competing' to do so." This could have major implications for teams like the Mavs, Spurs, and Grizzlies.

## Bridges' Motivation and Brunson's Success

In a fascinating twist, **Mikal Bridges** revealed that Becky Hammon's comments about Jalen Brunson motivated him to prove his doubters wrong. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared the quote: "I appreciate (Becky Hammon), no cap. The words she said about can’t win with (Jalen Brunson) being a ‘1A’, ‘1B’, ‘he too little’, all that did is fuel him bro." Brunson's championship win is a testament to his hard work and dedication.

## Coaching Carousel and Contract Concerns

The Spurs might be reevaluating their roster, and [RoyTarpleysGhost](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=881) wondered about the team's internal discussions regarding **DeAaron Fox**'s contract: "What kind of conversations are the Spurs are having internally about DeAaron Fox? He's owed like $220 million over the next 4 years. One of the worst contracts in the league?" Meanwhile, **Mike Brown**'s championship win has cemented his legacy as a top coach.

> "I tend to agree. Mike Brown has coached some pretty good teams before, and he still got fired. Lakers, Cavs (he was COY and won 66 games in 2009 and led them to the Finals before being swept by the Spurs)." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Mavs Fans React

As the NBA landscape shifts, Mavs fans are eager to see how their team will respond. Will Dallas make a splash in free agency or trades? One thing's for sure: the MavsBoard community will be keeping a close eye on all the action.

> "It's nice that the would-be dynasty has a very heavy albatross around their necks." - [Scott41theMavs](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=97)

The NBA offseason is just getting started, and we can't wait to see what the future holds for our beloved Mavs and the rest of the league!

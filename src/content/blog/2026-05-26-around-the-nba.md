## Knicks Are on Fire

The Knicks are absolutely cooking, and Mavs fans are feeling the burn. [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) kicked off the discussion with a scathing post about James Harden and Donovan Mitchell's performances: "Not even halftime and the Knicks have 26 fastbreak points. 16 2nd chance points. At this point Harden is a walking meme but Mitchell's effort might be even worse." The Knicks' impressive run has turned heads, and [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) gave them props: "Congrats to the Knicks. Everything is working for them now. They will be an underdog in the finals."

> "I must admit, I wasn't a big fan for most of the year, but they turned it on at the right time and look great." - [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404)

## Mavs Fans Regret Past Decisions

Meanwhile, Mavs fans are having flashbacks to past roster decisions. [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) brought up the Jalen Brunson fiasco: "Hot take, I am almost as mad with Cuban over the Jalen fiasco than I am with Nico on the Luka thing." [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) didn't directly respond, but it's clear that Mavs fans are reevaluating their team's roster construction.

## Western Conference Playoffs

The Western Conference playoffs are heating up, with the Spurs and OKC series tied. [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) shared their thoughts on the series: "First blowout of the series. Without Mitchell and Williams OKC doesn't have enough shot creation. Both teams struggled on offense but the Spurs don't rely on 3-point shooting and can play with brute force in the paint when nothing else is working." [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) had a different take: "OKC is just as battered with JDub and A.J. Mitchell out. OKC has the better depth, but those two are very important for them."

> "Double-teaming SGA hasn't worked thus far in the playoffs." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Knicks' Path to the Finals

The Knicks' path to the finals has been relatively easy, but [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) argued that they still deserve credit: "The Knicks really had an easier path through the playoffs than the other teams. That Atlanta series was their turning point when they went down 2-1. They rolled past Philly, and now Cleveland." [windjc](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=308) disagreed: "The Knicks are most likely getting swept by either team. Maybe they will last 5 or 6."

## Giannis and the NBA Rumors

In other news, [RoyTarpleysGhost](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=881) shared a report about Giannis Antetokounmpo's potential trade destinations: "The Boston Celtics and the Miami Heat are the two trade destinations that are currently believed to intrigue Giannis Antetokounmpo the most." This news has significant implications for the NBA landscape.

## Conclusion

As the playoffs continue, Mavs fans are left wondering what could've been. The Knicks are on a roll, and it's hard to deny their chemistry and momentum. Will they make a deep run in the finals? Only time will tell. One thing is clear: the Knicks are the team to beat right now.

> "I'll be interested to see if Presti makes any panic moves if the Thunder lose. Does he try and get out of Jalen Williams before an injury makes his contract untradeable?" - [RasheedsBigWhiteSpot](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=171)

The Knicks' dominance has Mavs fans talking, and we'll be keeping an eye on their journey. For now, let's just say: the Knicks are cooking, and Mavs fans are feeling the heat.

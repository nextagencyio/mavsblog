## Devin Booker's Jersey Switcheroo

Devin Booker is ditching his iconic #1 jersey for the #15, a nod to his USA Basketball uniform and his dad Melvin's college number. 
> "Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin" - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Luka's Blueprint

Luka Doncic's recent comments about needing shooters and a center who can run the pick-and-roll have Mavs fans excited. 
> "I think mainly shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll and jump so I can pass to him. If I have shooters, they won’t double me as much, and it helps me out." - Luka Doncic

## Laker Drama

The Lakers' offseason moves (or lack thereof) have everyone scratching their heads. 
> "Smart & Kennard are not Championship level players, not starters for sure. Maybe 10/15minutes role players." - [FireNicoHarrison](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=4633)

## Mavs' Center Search

The Mavs are looking to bolster their frontcourt, and [Mavs2021](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504) thinks they can get Daniel Gafford for the lowdown on the Lakers' 1st round pick. 
> "The guy that should literally be in prison is playing moral police." - [Mavs2021](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504)

## Spurs' Growing Pains

The Spurs' loss to the Warriors was disheartening, with Draymond Green calling out their lack of leadership. 
> "To see [the Spurs] walk off the court, it was disheartening. I’ll blame it on youth… There's a way to win, and there’s a way to lose. And walking off the court, not looking your killer in his eyes, isn’t the way to lose…" - Draymond Green

## Conference Realignment

The league is discussing realignment, with some teams potentially switching conferences. 
Memphis, Minnesota, and New Orleans are "competing" to move to the East. 

## MavsBoard Take

As the offseason heats up, Mavs fans are eager to see what GM Nico Harrison has up his sleeve. Will they land a top center, or will they have to get creative? One thing's for sure: with Luka at the helm, the Mavs are always a threat. And hey, if the Lakers are still trying to figure out their roster, that's just more ammo for the Mavs to take the West. **Prediction:** The Mavs will make a surprise move for a top free agent and make a deep playoff run.

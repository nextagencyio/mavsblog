## The Knicks' Thrilling Comeback

The MavsBoard community was on fire discussing the NBA Finals, and we're kicking off our roundup with the Knicks' thrilling comeback against the Spurs. [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) kicked off the conversation, saying: 
> "What a game. Knicks have that heart of a champion thing going on. I said the Spurs thought they were getting a coronation this series, instead they got a bar fight."

The Knicks' victory was fueled by clutch performances from Jalen Brunson and OG Anunoby. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared some key takeaways, including a quote from Brunson: 
> "There's nothing to celebrate. It's not over yet. Not even close"

## Spurs' Collapse: A Historic Choke?

The Spurs' 29-point lead evaporated in a stunning collapse. [SwisherPrice](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=254) had this to say: 
> "What a choke! Just an example of today's Live By the Three, Die By the Three NBA. Everything was fine in the 1st half when those threes were falling. But then they got too comfortable and kept taking threes, especially the ones early in the shot clock and with little ball movement/touching the paint first."

[Mavs2021](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504) wasn't afraid to troll rival fan [Nico](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=4633), saying: 
> "I just don´t see a Luka led Mavs losing to these teams. F*** Nico."

## A Finals MVP in the Making?

[HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) dropped some knowledge on OG Anunoby's impressive performance: 
> "OG Anunoby, you just hit the greatest shot in MSG and Knicks history in more than 50 years. How does it feel? 'It feels cool.'"

[Chuck](https://reddit) had a scathing take on the Spurs' collapse:

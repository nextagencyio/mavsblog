## Devin Booker's Jersey Switch

The NBA offseason is heating up, and Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is making a change. He's ditching his No. 1 jersey for No. 15, a nod to his USA Basketball uniform and his father's college number. 
> "Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin" - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Luka's Shooter SOS

In a recent interview, Luka Doncic shared what he needs from his teammates to perform at his best. 
> "I think mainly shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll and jump so I can pass to him. If I have shooters, they won’t double me as much, and it helps me out." - [Unknown](https://www.mavsboard.com)

## Knicks Parade and Jalen Brunson's Pay Cut

Over 2 million people attended the Knicks parade in lower Manhattan, celebrating their recent success. Meanwhile, Jalen Brunson's pay cut has sparked debate. 
> "The idea that any player making a contractual sacrifice automatically leads to success does not align with what I have seen repeatedly. Once a player sacrifices money, he is handing a tool back to an organization to make decisions he does not control, while still carrying the results of those decisions." - Bernie Lee 

[Becky Hammon's comments](https://www.mavsboard.com) on Jalen Brunson have also been a topic of discussion. Myles Bridges recently spoke on the matter, saying:
> "I appreciate (Becky Hammon), no cap. The words she said about can’t win with (Jalen Brunson) being a ‘1A’, ‘1B’, ‘he too little’, all that did is fuel him bro." - [The Big Lead](https://www.mavsboard.com)

## Conference Realignment and Coaching Moves

The NBA is exploring conference realignment, with some teams potentially switching conferences. The Spurs' recent loss to the Grizzlies has also sparked discussion on sportsmanship. 
> "To see [the Spurs] walk off the court, it was disheartening. I’ll blame it on youth. I’ll blame it on lacking the leader to show them that ‘hey, this is what you do. Not walk off.'" - Draymond Green 

In other coaching news, Chris Jent's name is circulating in the Blazers' coaching search.

## Devin Booker's New Number
Devin Booker is changing his jersey number from 1 to 15, a nod to his USA Basketball uniform and his father's college number. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared the news, which was reported by Shams Charania. 

> "Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin, per ESPN sources." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Luka's Needs
Luka Doncic had some insightful comments on what he needs from his teammates to succeed. In a YouTube video, Luka emphasized the importance of having shooters and a center who can run the pick-and-roll.

> "I think mainly shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll and jump so I can pass to him. If I have shooters, they won’t double me as much, and it helps me out." - Luka Doncic

## NBA Coaching Moves
The Portland Trail Blazers have found their new head coach in Micah Nori, who was the lead assistant for the Minnesota Timberwolves. [Mavs2021](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504) thinks this is a reasonable move, considering Nori's experience and age.

> "Nori is 52 years old. He has been an assistant for 17 years. I find this completely reasonable. He probably gets paid more than he did as an assistant in the last 17 years and he gets a chance to prove whatever he got as a head coach." - [Mavs2021](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504)

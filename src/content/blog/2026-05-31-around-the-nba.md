## The Spurs' Young Core is Unfair

The Spurs are on the rise, and Mavs fans are taking notice. **Chicagojk** [chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) points out that the Spurs have a talented young core, with seven players who played 10+ minutes in their recent game all under the age of 26. 

> "Will Guillory: Six of the seven Spurs who played 10+ minutes tonight are 26 or younger. These dudes are just getting started."

This young core is led by Wemby, who **CooPerFan** [cooperfan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=7574) believes might get his first ring soon, sparking "GOAT talk" as early as next season. 

## OKC's Draft Strategy

Meanwhile, OKC's draft strategy is being praised and criticized. **F Gump** [f-gump](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=412) breaks down how OKC acquired Mitchell in the draft, trading a back-of-the-roster guy and multiple picks to move up. 

> "True. And the way OKC was able to get Mitchell in the 1st place was GM wizardry... OKC trades a back of the roster guy for pick 52. They gave up a minimum salary guy (Waters) who they had developed from a free agent as a two-way for several years who they had just converted to a regular contract mid-season."

However, **HoosierDaddyKid** [hoosierdaddykid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) questions whether this strategy will pay off, asking "Ya think Presti knows what he's doing?"

## Knicks' Chances

The Knicks are also getting attention, with **HoosierDaddyKid** [hoosierdaddykid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) doubting their chances against the Spurs. 

> "I don't think the Knicks will beat them. KAT will be in a lot of foul trouble trying to guard Wemby. And the Spurs guards will cause plenty of havoc vs the Knicks guards including Brunson."

However, **Chicagojk** [chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) gives the Knicks a fighting chance, suggesting that they'll be a formidable opponent.

## Mavs Fans React

Mavs fans are still reeling from their team's performance. **FireNicoHarrison** [firenicoharrison](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=4633) expresses frustration, saying "I don't know if it's worse a player who Cuban gifted away wins the Finals MVP or the Spurs."

The Spurs' win has also sparked some Mavs fans to rank their favorite teams' chances. **Chicagojk** [chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) creates a "Finals team rage rankings" list, with the Rockets, Sixers, and Mavs making appearances.

## Conclusion

As the Spurs continue to rise and OKC falls, Mavs fans are left to wonder what could have been. With the Knicks and other teams vying for the title, the NBA landscape is shifting. One thing is for sure: Wemby and the Spurs are here to stay. 

Will the Mavs be able to compete with this young core? Only time will tell. But for now, **cow** [cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92) has a message for Mavs fans: "Castle is a dog and once he learns to harness his aggression, the league is in trouble."

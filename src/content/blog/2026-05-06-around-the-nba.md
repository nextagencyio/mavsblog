## Spurs' Coaching Conundrum

The MavsBoard community is buzzing with discussions about the NBA, and one of the hottest topics is the San Antonio Spurs' coaching situation. [**dirkfansince1998**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) expressed his frustration with the Spurs' decision-making in a recent game, saying:

> "I really dislike the T'Wolves but watching the Spurs lose because they don't have the IQ of Pop on the bench these days is a real feel good moment for Mavs fans. 7s left on the clock. Down 2. They don't call a timeout. And to make things worse they are slow to push the ball up the court."

This sentiment was met with some pushback from [**Mavs2021**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504), who argued that the Spurs' decision to go for the win instead of overtime wasn't that bad:

> "So your problem with the decision is that the Spurs did not make a wide open three. Gotcha."

However, [**dirkfansince1998**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) stood firm, saying:

> "My problem is them taking a three in the first place. There are higher percentage looks available if you only need two points to force overtime. But I guess teams going for the win instead of overtime is the norm these days."

## OKC's Dominance

Meanwhile, [**Chicagojk**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) was impressed by the Oklahoma City Thunder's performance, saying:

> "OKC is such a machine. You would think playing 11 in a playoff game would cause issues...this is missing their 2nd/3rd best player too. Nope, they just keep rolling."

He also praised the team's general manager for taking swings at talent acquisition:

> "My take on GM is find someone knows talent and then give them the resources (picks and other) to take as many swings as possible."

## Potential Offseason Moves

The discussion also touched on potential offseason moves, including the possibility of Jaylen Brown leaving the Boston Celtics. [**Unknown**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=0) shared a report that Brown is "deeply frustrated" with the Celtics organization, which could lead to some interesting developments:

> "Jaylen Brown is 'deeply frustrated' with the Boston Celtics organization, per @Tmac_213. 'I think his frustration lies deeply in the organization that we don't have the details to… There's just been a lot of stuff that I've been hearing, just going on with the Boston organization with JB.'"

Additionally, [**loki**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=390) suggested that the Dallas Mavericks could take a flyer on prospect Jordan Holland:

> "Seems like a great opportunity to take a flyer on Holland. The Mavs desperately need perimeter defenders and he's one of the best. He'll only be 21 next year so there's still time to develop the shot."

## MavsBoard Musings

Other discussions on the forum included [**RasheedsBigWhiteSpot**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=171) criticizing Magic's coach Mosley for his sideline demeanor:

> "I hate Moseley's sideline demeanor. There's so many times his team looked lost, especially in the halfcourt, and he'd just stand there with his arms crossed."

[**RoyTarpleysGhost**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=881) brought up an interesting point about Anthony Davis potentially being traded:

> "There's a legitimate chance that Anthony Davis is traded by the Wizards before appearing in a game for the franchise."

As the NBA offseason heats up, Mavs fans will be keeping a close eye on these storylines and potential moves. One thing's for sure: it's going to be an interesting few months.

## Hot Take

The Mavericks' best chance at making a deep playoff run next season lies in their ability to surround Luka Doncic with talent. With the right moves, they could become a legitimate contender. But if they fail to address their perimeter defense and playmaking issues, they might find themselves on the outside looking in once again. Only time will tell.

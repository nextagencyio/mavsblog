## Playoff Surprises

The playoffs have been full of surprises, and [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) is loving the Philly win in their series. When healthy, that Philly team is pretty good, and their two insanely quick guards cause fits for opposing teams.

> "When healthy, that Philly team is pretty good. I love having two insanely quick guards. They just cause so many fits." - [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404)

Meanwhile, the Magic's first-round exit has [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) calling for changes, starting with their coach. The team's half-court offense was dreadful, with no spacing, and it seemed like they were just mailing it in at times.

> "Barring a miracle that's three consecutive first round exits for the Magic. Still a young core but that's probably as far as Banchero will ever lead a team as a first option." - [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14)

## Mavs Musings

The Mavs may have dodged a bullet by not trading for Anthony Davis, according to some users. [RoyTarpleysGhost](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=881) shared that the Blazers offered a package centered around Jerami Grant, but it didn't gain traction.

> "It was Jerami Grant, expiring contracts, and a heavily protected pick for Anthony Davis, and it never gained any traction whatsoever." - [RoyTarpleysGhost](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=881)

And [Unknown](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=1016) chimed in, saying the most valuable part of the Wizards' deal was their ability to absorb money into their space.

## Trade Rumors and Targets

The NBA trade rumor mill is always churning, and [loki](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=390) thinks the Mavs should take a flyer on prospect Jonathan Holland.

> "Seems like a great opportunity to take a flyer on Holland. The Mavs desperately need perimeter defenders and he's one of the best." - [loki](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=390)

## Coaching Conundrums

The Magic's coach, Mosley, got roasted by [RasheedsBigWhiteSpot](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=171) for his sideline demeanor.

> "I hate Moseley's sideline demeanor. There's so many times his team looked lost, especially in the halfcourt, and he'd just stand there with his arms crossed." - [RasheedsBigWhiteSpot](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=171)

## Predictions and Takeaways

As the playoffs heat up, [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shared some key matchups to watch. In the Cavs-Raptors series, the pressure is on Cleveland to win at home and avoid a tumultuous offseason.

> "The Cavaliers have the highest-paid roster in the NBA with a superstar duo in Mitchell and Harden, who are trying to overcome their previous playoff disappointments." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

And finally, [Scott41theMavs](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=97) joked that Spida and Beard are riding JA's coattails into the second round.

The playoffs are unpredictable, but one thing's for sure - it's going to be a wild ride. Who will come out on top? Only time will tell.

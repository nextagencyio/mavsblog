## The Knicks are Back (or Are They?)
The Knicks are on a roll, but don't count on them to win it all just yet. [SwisherPrice](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=254) thinks they've got a good shot against the Spurs, but not OKC: "Knicks are really back. It's a game of matchups, I think they can beat the Spurs with the advantage in experience but not OKC." Meanwhile, [windjc](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=308) isn't buying it: "The Knicks are most likely getting swept by either team. Maybe they will last 5 or 6."

## Wemby's Woes
Wemby's having a tough time, and [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) isn't impressed: "I did not watch much of the game last night but from what I saw it was a very uncharacteristic game for Wemby. He needs to be better." And [numnuts23](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=573) has some... creative analysis: "Wemby got majority of his points at the line last night, just like SGA did. Wemby has learned to throw his head and arms around for the standing flop."

## The Thunder's On It
The Thunder are on a roll, and [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) has some juicy gossip: "A former NBA player accuses the refs of rigging the playoffs for the Thunder." Meanwhile, [RasheedsBigWhiteSpot](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=171) thinks OKC's success will impact their offseason moves: "If OKC kicks ass the rest of the way, he may be more apt to simply run it back than if his team loses by 30 in Game 5 and 6."

## Mavs Musings
The Mavs' offseason is already heating up, and [michaeltex](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=23) has some financial insight: "Sitting at $205M for 26/27 season with only 9 contracts. Currently sitting at $213M for 27/28 with only 7 contracts. OUCH." Meanwhile, [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) is still fuming about the team's playoff performance: "He highlighted the need to focus on transition defense in the pre-game interview only to watch his team give up 26 fastbreak points in the first half. Not just throwing the towel on the series. Doing it in a way that almost feels like they quit on the coach."

## Height Matters
The debate over height in the NBA continues, with [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) citing Becky Hammon: "WNBA coach Becky Hammon says if your best player is small you're not winning a title." But [mvossman](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=482) has some numbers to throw at that: "Steph was 6' 2" without shoes at 180 lbs and 6' 3.5" wingspan. I think that leans toward small." 

## The Takeaway
As the playoffs heat up, one thing's for sure: it's going to be a wild ride. From the Knicks' surprising run to Wemby's woes, there's plenty to talk about. And as [Winter](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=618) points out, "Wemby's body just doesn't look like the body that can withstand regular seasons, playoffs, and championships year-after-year." We'll see what the future holds, but for now, it's time to enjoy the ride.

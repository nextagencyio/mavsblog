# MavsBoard Roundup: AROUND the NBA

## Spurs' Late-Game Blunder
The Spurs' heartbreaking loss to the T'Wolves had [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) feeling some schadenfreude: "I really dislike the T'Wolves but watching the Spurs lose... is a real feel good moment for Mavs fans." He wasn't thrilled with San Antonio's decision-making down the stretch: "they don't call a timeout... I don't even understand why you would go for the winner in the first place." 

> "My problem is them taking a three in the first place. There are higher percentage looks available if you only need two points to force overtime. But I guess teams going for the win instead of overtime is the norm these days." - [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14)

[Mavs2021](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504) shot back, "So your problem with the decision is that the Spurs did not make a wide open three. Gotcha." But [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) stood firm, questioning the Spurs' strategy.

## NBA Playoff Surprises
The 76ers pulled off a surprising win in their series, which had [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) commenting: "When healthy, that Philly team is pretty good... They just cause so many fits." He loves their speedy guards, but notes that health is a major concern. 

The Celtics' impressive run wasn't entirely unexpected, but their early exit did raise some eyebrows.

## Pistons' Offseason Moves
[dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) shared some intel on the Pistons' potential offseason signings: "Not fixing their shooting or playmaking issues but if he signs for the BAE or less that's a great deal for the Pistons." The target player in question is a defensive specialist who's shown improvement in his shooting touch.

## Magic's Future
The Magic's early playoff exit might signal changes in Orlando: "Barring a miracle that's three consecutive first round exits for the Magic," noted [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14). He believes their young core needs a change in leadership: "they need to make some changes and it starts with the coach. Mosley is not it."

> "I hate Moseley's sideline demeanor. There's so many times his team looked lost, especially in the halfcourt, and he'd just stand there with his arms crossed." - [RasheedsBigWhiteSpot](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=171)

## Mavs Rumors & More
In other news, [loki](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=390) suggested the Mavs take a flyer on a young prospect: "Seems like a great opportunity to take a flyer on Holland. The Mavs desperately need perimeter defenders and he's one of the best."

The Blazers' pursuit of Giannis Antetokounmpo and potential interest in Anthony Davis had [RoyTarpleysGhost](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=881) sharing some intel: "There's a legitimate chance that Anthony Davis is traded by the Wizards before appearing in a game for the franchise."

## Hot Takes & Predictions
As the playoffs heat up, [Scott41theMavs](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=97) joked that the Jazz are going to ride JA's coattails into the second round: "Looks like Spida and Beard are going to ride JA's coattails into the second round." 

One thing's for sure – the NBA is full of surprises. Will the 76ers stay healthy and make a deep run? Can the young Magic core bounce back next season? One prediction's for sure: Mavs fans will be keeping a close eye on it all.

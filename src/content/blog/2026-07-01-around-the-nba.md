## MavsBoard Roundup: AROUND the NBA

## **The Booker Bump**
Devin Booker is changing his jersey number from 1 to 15. I mean, who wouldn't want to ditch the number 1 after being part of a team that got swept in the playoffs? According to [Shams Charania](https://twitter.com/ShamsCharania), Booker is making the switch to honor his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin. 

> "Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin, per ESPN sources." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## **The Blazers' Coaching Conundrum**
The Portland Trail Blazers are still searching for a head coach, and it seems like they're considering some big names. [Marc Stein](https://twitter.com/MarcStein) reported that the Blazers are looking at Knicks assistant Chris Jent, along with finalists Micah Nori (Wolves) and Tyler Lashbrook (Celtics). But in a surprise move, Micah Nori agreed to become the head coach of the Portland Trail Blazers, signing a one-year deal with team options for each of the next two seasons.

> "BREAKING: Micah Nori has agreed to become the head coach of the Portland Trail Blazers, sources tell @TheAthletic . Nori was lead assistant of the Minnesota Timberwolves for the past five years." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## **Luka's Blueprint**
Luka Doncic had some interesting comments on what he needs from his teammates to succeed. According to Luka, he needs shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll and jump high. 

> "I think mainly shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll and jump so I can pass to him. If I have shooters, they won’t double me as much, and it helps me out." - [Luka Doncic](https://www.youtube.com)

## **The Mavs' Offseason**
The Mavs are still hiring their coaching staff, and it seems like they've added some new faces. [Sean Sweeney](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/4921...)’s coaching staff has begun to take shape with HoopsHype reporting the additions of DJ Bakker, Mfon Udofia, and Riley Crean as assistant coaches.

## **Jalen Brunson's Pay Cut**
Jalen Brunson made headlines recently for taking a pay cut with the Knicks. But according to [Bernie Lee](https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/4921...), making a contractual sacrifice doesn't automatically lead to success.

> "The idea that any player making a contractual sacrifice automatically leads to success does not align with what I have seen repeatedly." - [Bernie Lee](https://twitter.com/)


## **NBA News Bites**
Some other news from around the league: 
- Damon Jones is listed as a co-conspirator in the Ed Davis gambling case 
- Mark Williams, a free agent this season, currently wears No. 15 in Phoenix 
- The Knicks parade drew over 2 million people to lower Manhattan 
- Gary Payton had a street named after him in his hometown of Oakland 

## **Hot Take**
As the offseason heats up, one thing is clear: the Mavs need to surround Luka with shooters and a solid big man. And maybe, just maybe, we should be worried about the Blazers' new coach, Micah Nori - will he be able to turn the team around? Only time will tell. For now, I'm just excited to see what the Mavs do next. And if you're not, well, you're just not paying attention.

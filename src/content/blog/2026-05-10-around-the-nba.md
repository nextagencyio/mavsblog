## The Wemby vs. Flagg Debate Rages On

The MavsBoard community is still buzzing about the Victor Wemby vs. Cooper Flagg debate. For those who may have missed it, Wemby is a 7'5" phenom who's dominating the league, while Flagg is a highly touted prospect with a lot of potential. [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) sparked the discussion with a bold claim: "Bottom line is that even when Wemby is 5/17 from the field he is arguably the most impactful player on the floor. That's how good his defense is."

> "Wemby does things that are unfathomable for someone 7'5.  If he can stay healthy, Wemby is one of one and can be one of the greatest to ever play the game." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Cade's Slump and the Harden Comparison

The discussion also turned to Cade Cunningham, who's been struggling with turnovers. [Mavs2021](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504) joked that Cade's mistakes made James Harden think he's Michael Jordan. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) came to Cade's defense, suggesting that fatigue might have been a factor: "He led them to come back down 16 pts. I mean, he's susceptible to turnovers. All players who have the ball consistently are."

## OKC's Future and the Lottery

The conversation shifted to the NBA lottery, with [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) predicting that OKC will snag the #1 pick and inevitably disappoint. [Winter](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=618) chimed in with a pessimistic view of OKC's future: "People think OKC is going to be good for a long time.... but they're just 15 injuries away from being average."

## Coaching News and Lakers Drama

In other news, Steve Kerr has agreed to a new two-year contract with the Golden State Warriors. Meanwhile, [CooPerFan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=7574) is convinced that the Lakers need Luka Doncic to get at least one game in their series.

## BBIQ and Wemby's Game

The discussion also touched on Wemby's Basketball IQ (BBIQ). [CooPerFan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=7574) suggested that Wemby needs to work on his BBIQ, while [hakeemfaan](https://://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=499) pushed back: "I don't want to argue on this.  We just disagree.  People  casually use the term BBIQ without even understanding what it fully means."

## The Verdict

As the discussion comes to a close, one thing is clear: the MavsBoard community is passionate about their basketball. Whether it's Wemby vs. Flagg or Cade's slump, there's no shortage of opinions. As [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) so aptly put it: "Obviously much better to pretend that Wemby isn't as good as he is. Especially if you want to beat him at some point in the not so distant future." The debate will continue, but for now, let's just enjoy the ride. 

And if you're [CooPerFan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=7574), just remember: if Wemby develops a mid-range fadeaway, the league is in trouble.

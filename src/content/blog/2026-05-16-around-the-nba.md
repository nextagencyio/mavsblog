## The Spurs' Rise and Mavs Fans' Guilt
[cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92) kicked off the discussion with a candid confession: "Feels dirty cheering for the Spurs." This sparked a mix of reactions, with some fans embracing the Spurs' resurgence and others expressing discomfort. 
> "Feels dirty cheering for the Spurs." - [cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92)

## Coaching Carousel and Potential Mavs Moves
The possibility of Nick Nurse joining the Mavs' coaching staff or Jason Kidd heading to Philadelphia was floated by [DanSchwartzgan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=72). 
> "Straight up swap...Kidd to the Philly FO and Nurse to the Dallas bench?" - [DanSchwartzgan](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=72)

## Playoff Drama: Pistons' Collapse and Brunson's Rise
The Detroit Pistons' stunning collapse against the Cleveland Cavaliers was a major topic, with [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) lamenting: 
> "That was brutal to watch. Detroit with an epic choke job in the last three minutes of the 4th quarter and completly out of it in OT." 

Meanwhile, [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) marveled at Jalen Brunson's performance: 
> "Winner. Brunson. Can’t believe this franchise had a draft like they had but we can never look back and enjoy it." 

## What Ifs: Luka and Brunson Together
The discussion also turned to what could have been if Brunson and Luka Dončić had played together. 
> "So, do you think Brunson and Luka could have co-existed? I mean, it was stated that Brunson wanted the opportunity to run his own team, not play second fiddle to Luka." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## League Reforms and Lottery Changes
The NBA's new lottery reform and referee rating system were also debated. 
> "The expectation around the NBA is the new lottery reform will only last three years from 2027-2029." 

## In Conclusion
As the playoffs heat up and the offseason approaches, Mavs fans have plenty to discuss. One thing's for sure: the Spurs' rise, coaching changes, and playoff drama will continue to dominate the conversation. Will the Mavs make any significant moves? Only time will tell. One thing's for certain, though – Mavs fans will be watching closely.

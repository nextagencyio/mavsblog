## Devin Booker's Jersey Switch
Devin Booker is ditching his iconic No. 1 jersey for No. 15, a nod to his USA Basketball uniform and his dad Melvin's college number. 
> "Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin" - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## NBA Coaching Carousel
The Portland Trail Blazers have found their new head coach in Micah Nori, a 52-year-old assistant coach with 17 years of experience. 
> "Micah Nori has agreed to become the head coach of the Portland Trail Blazers, sources tell @TheAthletic . Nori was lead assistant of the Minnesota Timberwolves for the past five years." 
Nori's hiring leaves the Dallas Mavericks' coaching staff, led by Jason Kidd, looking pretty solid in comparison.

## Luka's Ideal Teammates
Luka Doncic had a chat about what he needs from his teammates to shine on the court. 
> "I think mainly shooters and a big man who can run the pick-and-roll and jump so I can pass to him," 
It sounds like Luka's wishlist includes sharpshooters and a big man who can roll with him.

## Knicks' Parade and Offseason
The New York Knicks' championship parade drew a massive crowd of over 2 million people, but one attendee's antics stole the show - a woman who was caught on video emptying a trash can and stealing it. 
> "A woman caught on video emptying a public trash can on the street, then stealing it during New York City’s Knicks championship parade, was a director at JPMorgan Chase who was fired Tuesday over the incident" 
As for the team's offseason, Jalen Brunson's pay cut has raised some eyebrows. 
> "The idea that any player making a contractual sacrifice automatically leads to success does not align with what I have seen repeatedly." - Bernie Lee 

## MavsBoard Takes
Some MavsBoard users are still fuming about the Celtics' championship win without [FireNicoHarrison](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=4633) claiming that former Celtics star Marcus Smart was "overrated." 
> "Boston won a ring without his 'soul and heart' because a PG/SG who can't shoot can't be a Championship starter in this league."
Meanwhile, [Mavs2021](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504) thinks Micah Nori's hiring in Portland is a solid move. 
> "Nori is 52 years old. He has been an assistant for 17 years. I find this completely reasonable. He probably gets paid more than he did as an assistant in the last 17 years and he gets a chance to prove whatever he got as a head coach."

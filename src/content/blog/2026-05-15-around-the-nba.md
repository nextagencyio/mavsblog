## Coaching Carousel
The MavsBoard community is buzzing about the potential swap of Jason Kidd to the Philadelphia front office and Nick Nurse to the Dallas bench. [**DanSchwartzgan**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=72) kicks off the discussion with a straightforward proposal: 

> "Straight up swap...Kidd to the Philly FO and Nurse to the Dallas bench?"

[**Chicagojk**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) chimes in, recalling the reportedly tumultuous relationship between Nurse and Masai Ujiri:

## NBA Playoff Analysis
The community is also dissecting the NBA playoffs, particularly the Detroit Pistons' collapse against the Cleveland Cavaliers. [**dirkfansince1998**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) expresses his frustration: 

> "That was brutal to watch. Detroit with an epic choke job in the last three minutes of the 4th quarter and completly out of it in OT. Harden and Mitchell trying their best to throw it in OT but even their best effort wasn't enough. Detroit refused to accept the gift."

[**HoosierDaddyKid**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) analyzes the Pistons' shortcomings, citing their lack of shooters and Cade Cunningham's heavy workload: 

> "Cade is a great player, but he has been on a turnover binge, and he has to do everything for them. Duren has disappeared, and Tobias Harris has played well but came up short last night."

## Player News
The Indiana Pacers made headlines by signing Ivica Zubac to a $42.1 million extension over two seasons. [**HoosierDaddyKid**](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) provides context: 

> "At 29, Ivica Zubac should be in his prime. The Pacers likely needed to pay a premium for his deal, which will pay him just $42.1 million over the next two seasons."

Meanwhile, Luka Doncic announced he won't play for Slovenia's national team this summer to focus on securing joint custody of his daughters. [**Unknown**](https://www.mavsboard.com) shares the news: 

> "The Lakers’ Luka Dončić announced tonight via Instagram that he will not play for Slovenia's national team this summer in FIBA World Cup qualifying to spend as much offseason time as possible with his two daughters as he continues to try to secure joint custody."

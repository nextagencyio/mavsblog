## OKC's Championship Window and Salary Cap Woes

The OKC Thunder are on the rise, and their recent performance has fans excited. However, [windjc](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=308) raises a valid concern about the team's salary cap situation:

> "So lucky they are immune to the NBA salary cap rules though. And after they win the championship this year no player on that roster is going to take LESS money to resign. Other teams will offer more for championship winners."

The Thunder's young core, led by SGA, has been impressive, but their salary cap situation could become a major issue soon. With SGA's massive increase kicking in next year, things could get complicated. Will they be able to keep their roster intact?

## Wemby's Growth and the Spurs' Playoff Run

The Spurs' Victor Wemby has been a topic of discussion, with [cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92) suggesting he needs to work on his post-game:

> "Wemby needs to get in the lab over the summer because you have to abuse being defended by someone of Caruso's stature in the post."

Despite his impressive skills, Wemby's performance in the playoffs has been inconsistent. [Knutsen](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=963) offers some advice:

> "They need to give him the ball moving and rolling to the basket much more. And the deadliest thing would be if Dirk mentors him on killing it from the elbow - positioning, catching, turning, faking, shooting, sometimes taking a single dribble."

## Mavs Musings and Trade Rumors

The Mavs' faithful are always looking for ways to improve their team. [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) thinks they should target Harrison Barnes:

> "Mavs need to find a Harrison Barnes trade. Not for what he can do on the court. San Antonio got Barnes and the right to swap picks with the Kings in 2031."

Barnes might not be the most exciting player, but he's a true professional. The Mavs might need to make some moves to stay competitive in the Western Conference.

## Playoff Takeaways and Predictions

The playoffs have been full of surprises, and [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) has some thoughts on the Spurs' and OKC's series:

> "Spurs losing their cool. OKC getting exactly what they want. Physical. Dirty. But effective."

As the playoffs heat up, we'll see which teams can adapt and make a deep run. One thing's for sure: the Mavs faithful will be watching closely, hoping their team makes a move soon.

## The Knicks' Gritty Win

The Knicks are on the doorstep of a championship, and [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) is loving every minute of it. Despite being hated by many, the Knicks' win in Game 4 was inspiring for basketball fans. 
> "The Spurs need a heartbreak to prep for their eventual Championship, and tonight was it. Every great team has gone through this. They'll cry in the locker room tonight and be better for it in the coming seasons." - [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159)

## Spurs' Physicality and Wemby's Growth

The Spurs' physicality has been a topic of discussion, with [michaeltex](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=23) criticizing Wemby's thuggery during the playoffs. 
> "I've always thought Wemby was a spectacular, once in a generation, talent. But I have to say my appreciation of his game has been tarnished by some of the thuggery he's displayed during these playoffs. Taunting, unnecessary force, headshots, elbows, flops, etc." - [michaeltex](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=23)

## Mavs' Future and Young Core

The Mavs' young core, consisting of players like [cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92)'s favorites Castle and Harper, has Mavs fans excited for the future. 
> "Even without Wemby, I'd say the future is pretty bright with Castle/Harper and that combo can let Wemby not have to log major minutes in the regular season which should help him stay healthier." - [cow](https://https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92)

## James Dolan and the Knicks' Ownership

[Mavs fans aren't the only ones weighing in on the NBA Finals;](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) is annoyed by James Dolan's behavior. 
> "Bad owners make everything hopeless. What is sucks is he has been terrible for the Knicks but is now sitting on the doorstep of a championship and gets all the glory." - [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404)

## The Playoffs: A Throwback to the Late 90s/Early 00s?

The NBA playoffs have been a wild ride so far, folks. **dirkfansince1998** [https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14] kicked off the discussion, saying:

> "Highest scoring regular season in forever followed by playoffs that feel more like a throwback to the late 90s/early 00s."

He's right; the playoffs have been a slugfest, with teams struggling to score and refs allowing more contact. But what's behind this trend? Is it good defense, or are teams just not spacing the floor properly?

## Wemby: A Generational Talent?

**HoosierDaddyKid** [https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159] weighed in on Victor Wembanyama's impact:

> "Wemby is a generational talent on both ends of the court. Cooper is good, but not a game changer like Wemby is."

Meanwhile, **CooPerFan** [https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=7574] isn't sold on Wemby's offense:

> "Still not sold on wemby’s offense. I still think cooper will be a much better player than wemby."

It's clear that Wemby is making waves, but will he live up to the hype?

## Coaching and Front Office Moves

**Chicagojk** [https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404] praised the OKC front office:

> "OKC is such a machine. You would think playing 11 in a playoff game would cause issues...this is missing their 2nd/3rd best player too. Nope, they just keep rolling."

He also shared his take on finding talent:

> "My take on GM is find someone knows talent and then give them the resources (picks and other) to take as many swings as possible."

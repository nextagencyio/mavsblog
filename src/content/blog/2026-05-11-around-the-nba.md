## Lottery Love and Wizards' Windfall
The NBA lottery has come and gone, and [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) is calling it like he saw it: "I called it, Wizards got the 1st pick." The Wizards' dire guard situation might just get a boost with the top pick, and [Mavs2021](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=504) has a theory on how the draft might shake out: 
> "If Washington takes Peterson, which seems plausible considering their dire guard situation, then Utah gets the guy that Ainge paid $10M to play highschool/college in Utah. Memphis gets their grit and grind style franchise player mixture of Zach Randolph and Marc Gasol."

Meanwhile, [cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92) is just happy that the Lottery Ball Gods are smiling upon the Mavs once again: "Lottery Ball Gods have resumed their normally scheduled programming when it comes to the Mavs. All is right in the world."

## Playoff Drama and Disappointment
The playoffs have been a wild ride, and [Chicagojk](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=404) is feeling the pain of a sweep: "Terrible feeling after such a fun round 1 win to go out and lose in a sweep and just get destroyed. Yuck." The 76ers' effort level has been a point of contention, with [dirkfansince1998](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=14) calling it "absolutely pathetic."

## Wembanyama's Elbow and Enforcer Needs
Victor Wembanyama's elbow to the face has sparked a heated debate, with [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) weighing in on the potential consequences: 
> "He can only hope the league doesn't tack on additional suspension time. But I doubt it happens. Wemby is one of the future faces of the league."

[cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92) isn't opposed to a little extra punishment: "I don't think it happens either, but I wouldn't be upset if it did. Shades of Metta World Peace on that elbow." [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) thinks Wemby needs an enforcer by his side: "Wemby needs an enforcer next to him, like a Charles Oakley. SA has nobody on their team that fits that role."

## Ref Calls and Replay Drama
The refs have been under scrutiny, and [david75090](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=431) thinks they got some calls wrong: 
> "Thought Wemby was fouled, then he was fouled while he threw the elbow. The elbow throwing was called correctly, him being fouled before, after and during his throwing of the elbow was not called correctly."

## A Look Ahead
As the playoffs heat up, teams are looking to make a push for the championship. [JBB](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=868) points to OKC as an example of a team building for the future: 
> "Agreed. OKC is the perfect example with Holmgren/Hartenstein."

And with that, the MavsBoard community is buzzing with excitement and debate. Will the Wizards take Peterson with the top pick? Can Wemby and the Spurs make a deep playoff run? One thing's for sure: Mavs fans will be keeping a close eye on it all. 

Prediction: The Wizards take a swing at Peterson, and Wemby gets a (small) fine for his elbow shenanigans. What do you think, Mavs fans?

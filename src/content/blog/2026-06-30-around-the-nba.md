## MavsBoard Roundup: AROUND the NBA

## The NBA's Got a Gambling Problem
The league is dealing with some off-court issues, and [cow](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=92) brings it up with a post about Ed Davis and Damon Jones being linked to a gambling case: 
> "Ed Davis too. Damon Jones is listed a co-conspirator: https://www.espn.com/nba/story/_/id/4921...bling-case"

And it gets even more serious with news of Malik Beasley being indicted on federal charges for gambling related to a sports betting scheme. This isn't his first run-in, with some users wondering if this is connected to a situation from a year or two ago involving poker games.

## A Tribute to The Mailman
Gary Payton, aka "The Mailman," is getting the recognition he deserves. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shares that a street was named after him in his hometown of Oakland. 
> "Marc J. Spears: Naismith Basketball Hall of Famer @GaryPayton had a street named after him in his hometown @Oakland today."

## What's in a Jersey Number?
Devin Booker is making a change to his jersey number, switching from No. 1 to No. 15. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) mentions that this is in honor of his USA Basketball uniform and his father's college number. 
> "Shams Charania: Phoenix Suns star Devin Booker is changing his jersey from No. 1 to No. 15, honoring his USA Basketball uniform and the college number of his father, Melvin, per ESPN sources."

Interestingly, Mark Williams, a free agent this season who currently wears No. 15 for the Phoenix Suns, might have some competition for that number.

## Luka's Recipe for Success
Luka Doncic had some insightful comments on what he needs from his teammates to perform at his best. 
> "Luka Dončić: Well, I think above all, shooters and a center who can run the pick and roll and jump in so I can pass to him. And if I have shooters, they can't double-team me as much, so it helps me a lot."

It sounds simple, but having shooters and a mobile big man could make a world of difference for the Mavs.

## Coaching Carousel
The NBA coaching world is seeing some changes, with Micah Nori agreeing to become the head coach of the Portland Trail Blazers. [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shares the news: 
> "Marc Stein: Dallas’ hiring of Dusty May leaves Portland as the NBA’s only team without a head coach entering Tuesday’s draft … with sources saying Knicks assistant Chris Jent is getting real consideration for that post alongside finalists Micah Nori (Wolves) and Tyler Lashbrook (Celtics)."

## A Look Back at the Celtics' Success
There's been some discussion about the Celtics' recent championship win and what contributed to their success. 
> "FireNicoHarrison: Boston won a ring without his 'soul and heart' because a PG/SG who can't shoot can't be a Championship starter in this league."

It seems like some users are still trying to make sense of the Celtics' roster moves and how they led to their championship.

## Jalen Brunson's Pay Cut
[Jalen Brunson](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=352) took a pay cut to stay with the Knicks, but [HoosierDaddyKid](https://www.mavsboard.com/member.php?action=profile&uid=159) shares a nuanced view: 
> "Bernie Lee on Jalen Brunson’s pay cut with Knicks: The idea that any player making a contractual sacrifice automatically leads to success does not align with what I have seen repeatedly."

It's a reminder that success in the NBA is never a guarantee, no matter what financial sacrifices players make.

## Mavs Musing
As we wrap up this roundup, it's hard not to think about what could be in store for the Mavs next season. With Luka's requirements in mind, will the team make some moves to surround him with shooters and a mobile big man? Only time will tell.

But one thing's for sure: the NBA is full of surprises, and Mavs fans are along for the ride. As Luka would say, bring on the shooters and centers who can jump high and block shots!
